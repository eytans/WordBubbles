WordBubbles: Learn & Play - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

Introduction

WordBubbles: Learn & Play ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and their families. This privacy policy explains how our mobile application handles information when you use our service.

Information We Do Not Collect

WordBubbles is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users, including:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Audio recordings or speech data
  • Any other personal information

How the App Works

The packaged Android app operates offline on your device. The app:

  • Uses your device's text-to-speech functionality to pronounce words
  • Displays locally stored images that are included with the app to create a visually engaging experience
  • Keeps game progress in the current app session; it is cleared when the app is restarted
  • Does not require an internet connection to function after installation

Third-Party Services

The packaged Android app does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, or external application services. Its images, word content, and music are bundled locally. The optional web build is hosted on GitHub Pages and may download Flutter's web runtime assets, including CanvasKit, during its first load; the web build does not collect WordBubbles user data.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

WordBubbles is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We:

  • Do not collect personal information from children under 13
  • Do not require registration or account creation
  • Do not include social features or communication capabilities
  • Do not include advertising or in-app purchases
  • The packaged Android app does not connect to the internet or external application services

Data Security

Since WordBubbles does not collect or store personal information, no WordBubbles user data is transmitted to or stored on our servers. Android app functionality and data remain on the device; the web build's hosting and Flutter runtime downloads do not include WordBubbles account or analytics data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
